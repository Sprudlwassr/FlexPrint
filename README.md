\# Diploma thesis FlexPrint


This diploma thesis develops a modular CoreXY 3D printer that flexibly combines mechanics, electronics, and software and is designed for future extensions. The mechanical setup includes a Z-axis with four individually driven motors, linear rails, and a magnetically interchangeable toolhead system for different nozzle diameters. Electronically, a custom mainboard based on ESP32 or STM32 with TMC2209 stepper motor drivers is implemented. Control is provided via customized firmware (Klipper or Marlin). Extensions such as alternative toolheads, modular interfaces, and a secure power supply are integrated. The goal is a practical, reliable 3D printer that goes beyond commercial devices and serves as a platform for research and development.
